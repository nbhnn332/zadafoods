2. Inventory Stock Thresholds
  • In Stock (>10 units): Normal sales active on website.
  • Low Stock (1-10 units): Automatic alert on dashboard; schedule repacking.
  • Out of Stock (0 units): Storefront disables "Add to Cart" automatically.

To adjust stock: Go to Inventory > Click stock quantity > Update value > Click Checkmark.

3. Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules
  • Mandatory Advance: Token advance (₹60 or 15%) paid online via Razorpay before dispatch.
  • Invoice Notice: Package label clearly displays remaining doorstep balance.
  • Courier Remittance: Weekly courier payout must equal doorstep collected balance.
